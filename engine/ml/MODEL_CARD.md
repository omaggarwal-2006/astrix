> [!CAUTION]
> ### The Honest Frame: Clinical Disclaimer & Transparency
> **This is a small model trained on synthetic data, illustrative, not a clinical predictor.**  
> - **No Real Astronaut Data:** NASA’s public pages provide rich research text, hazard descriptions, and aggregate study statistics, but **no raw astronaut health datasets**. We do not claim access to any classified or private flight telemetry. All training data is generated by our own physiological simulator (`simulator.py`).
> - **Simulator Learning Caveat:** A machine learning model trained on its own simulator naturally learns the rules of that simulator. To evaluate the model honestly, we:
>   1. Validated on **held-out simulated astronauts whose parameters were shifted** (out-of-distribution susceptibility, baseline shifts, randomized anomaly timings).
>   2. Benchmarked directly against our simple **rule-based forecast** (NASA published rate formulas) and reported honestly where the ML model is better, equal, or worse.
> - **SANS Screening Boundary:** LANDFALL provides decision support, not autonomous clinical diagnosis. Any SANS (Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome) symptom triggers a mandatory instruction to *"Report to the flight surgeon"*.

## 1. Model Overview

- **Model Architecture:** Multi-target $L_2$-Regularized Ridge Regression with Calibrated Quantile Residual Uncertainty Bands (10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles).
- **Model Size:** **7.52 KB** (`engine/model.json`), well below the 1 MB hackathon requirement.
- **Runtime Environment:** 100% offline, zero-dependency browser and Node.js inference engine (`engine/predict.js`). Runs in microseconds without WebAssembly, Python, or network dependencies.
- **Targets (Landing-Day 180 Readiness):**
  1. `bone`: Bone Mineral Density Retention (%)
  2. `muscle`: Musculoskeletal Strength Retention (%)
  3. `aerobic`: Cardiorespiratory Aerobic Retention (%)
  4. `balance`: Neurovestibular Balance Retention (%)
  5. `sleep`: Circadian Sleep Readiness Score (0–100)
  6. `mood`: Behavioral Mood & Cognitive Resilience Score (0–100)

## 3. Evaluation & Honest Baseline Comparison

We evaluated the model on **30 held-out Shifted Parameter Astronauts** (480 evaluation windows) whose baselines, stress triggers, and loss rates differed from training:

```text
================================================================================
HONEST PERFORMANCE COMPARISON ON HELD-OUT SHIFTED TEST GROUP (OOD)
Tested on 30 unseen astronauts under shifted stress & susceptibility parameters
================================================================================
Target Dimension | ML MAE   | Rule MAE  | ML RAG %  | Rule RAG % | Verdict 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
bone             | 0.915    | 0.843     | 97.3    % | 96.0     % | Equal   
muscle           | 1.329    | 1.762     | 86.7    % | 84.0     % | Better  
aerobic          | 1.256    | 2.866     | 79.8    % | 69.6     % | Better  
balance          | 0.342    | 5.682     | 79.6    % | 78.3     % | Better  
sleep            | 0.736    | 5.881     | 100.0   % | 90.0     % | Better  
mood             | 2.657    | 10.189    | 98.1    % | 76.2     % | Better  
```

### Why the Differences Occur:
1. **Bone Density (Verdict: Equal):** Bone resorption in microgravity follows a largely steady rate. The rule-based formula ($1.25\%/\text{month}$) is already mathematically close to linear truth; ML achieves comparable error without overcomplicating the projection.
2. **Muscle & Aerobic (Verdict: Better):** ML captures personal baseline differences, resting vitals drift, and historical exercise trajectory, whereas rule-based models assume a population average.
3. **Sleep & Mood (Verdict: Better without Strawman):** Even when comparing against a dynamic rolling heuristic rule (rather than a hardcoded Day-40 assumption), the ML model significantly outperforms ($100.0\%$ vs. $90.0\%$ on sleep, $98.1\%$ vs. $76.2\%$ on mood, cutting MAE by $>50\%$). This is because the ML model integrates **cumulative CUSUM sleep debt** and the **delayed multi-hazard lag** (sleep collapse degrading mood 3 days later, coupled with cabin CO₂ elevation), which a naive rolling window cannot capture.

### Physical Monotonicity Verification:
- **Exercise What-If Sweep ($0.0 \to 1.5\text{ h/day}$):** Tested across all 6 dimensions. **6/6 passed monotonicity**. More daily exercise adherence strictly maintains or improves readiness, never penalizing the astronaut.

## 4. UI Contract & Explainability (`predict.js`)

The engine exports four stable methods to Om’s UI:

1. `predictLanding(history, options)`: Returns 6-dimensional profile `{ low, mid, high, status }` (`"green"`, `"amber"`, `"red"`).
2. `whatIf(history, exerciseHoursPerDay)`: Real-time slider recalculation in $<1\text{ ms}$.
3. `explain(history)`: Plain-words feature attribution using the model’s mathematical weights ($w_i \cdot z_i$), e.g.:
   > *"7-day sleep averaged 4.7 h vs your baseline of 7.4 h (2.7 h deficit), which is the leading factor influencing sleep and mood resilience projections."*
4. `modelInfo()`: Returns version, citations, and the explicit *"Illustrative, not a clinical predictor"* disclaimer.

## 5. Limitations

- **Simulator Dependent:** Validated only on synthetic physics and physiological couplings.
- **Balance Measure Confidence:** Lowest confidence dimension due to the lack of continuous daily sensorimotor sensors.
- **Offline Safety Fallback:** If `model.json` fails to load, `predict.js` automatically routes calls to `ForecastEngine` to prevent UI disruption.
